================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================
Guide Kenyans and Africans in the German diaspora on business creation,
diaspora investment, and grant opportunities. This SOP covers the WIDU.africa
grant programme in detail, the AG-Career Hub Diaspora Investment Desk, real
estate investment guidance, and pathways to entering European markets with
African products.

================================================================================
SCOPE
================================================================================
INCLUDED:
- WIDU.africa diaspora grant programme (full process)
- Diaspora Investment Desk: real estate Kenya, financial investments Germany
- European market entry for African agri-food products
- Self-employment and freelancing in Germany (limited; see SOP-006)
- MuTech Technical Training Institute partnership for skills supply

EXCLUDED:
- Corporate tax advice (refer to WTS or specialised tax advisors)
- Venture capital or angel investment (beyond this SOP's scope)
- Business registration in Kenya (refer to Kenya Revenue Authority / Business
  Registration Service directly)

================================================================================
PREREQUISITES
================================================================================
For WIDU.africa:
  P-1: User must be part of the African diaspora living in one of the following
       countries: Germany, France, Switzerland, Austria, the Netherlands, Norway,
       Belgium, or Sweden
  P-2: User must have an entrepreneur contact based in Kenya, Ethiopia, Ghana,
       Cameroon, Togo, or Tunisia
  P-3: Entrepreneur must operate a micro or small business (existing or to be started)
  P-4: Both donor and entrepreneur must be willing to invest private funds upfront
       before any WIDU grant is released

PROCEDURE
================================================================================

SECTION A: WIDU.AFRICA GRANT PROGRAMME

STEP A-1 — UNDERSTAND THE WIDU MODEL
  WIDU.africa is an initiative by:
  - The German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ)
  - Implemented by GIZ (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit)
  
  Core model:
  - A diaspora member (called the DONOR) residing in Europe invites an
    entrepreneur based in Africa to apply for a grant
  - The DONOR provides part of the upfront investment and offers moral or
    strategic business support
  - The ENTREPRENEUR applies for the WIDU grant and receives both funding
    and coaching if approved
  
  AG-Career Hub is a certified WIDU.africa advisory partner since 2023,
  specifically for the Kenyan community in Germany.

STEP A-2 — CONFIRM ELIGIBILITY
  DONOR (diaspora member in Germany) must:
  - Reside in Germany (or other eligible European country)
  - Be of African origin (diaspora definition applies)
  - Be willing to co-invest a portion of the grant amount upfront
  - Provide advisory/moral support to the entrepreneur

  ENTREPRENEUR (in Kenya or other eligible country) must:
  - Be based in Kenya, Ethiopia, Ghana, Cameroon, Togo, or Tunisia
  - Operate or intend to operate a micro/small business
  - Be willing to invest their own funds upfront and maintain transparent
    financial records

STEP A-3 — UNDERSTAND THE APPLICATION PROCESS
  3a. DONOR INITIATES the application process by:
      - Registering on the WIDU.africa platform
      - Inviting the entrepreneur to apply
      - Committing to co-invest their portion

  3b. ENTREPRENEUR submits a detailed INVESTMENT PLAN covering:
      - Business description and justification
      - Chronological list of planned expenditures
      - How the WIDU grant funds will be used
      - Supporting market prices and quotes

  3c. WIDU team reviews the application and investment plan
  3d. If approved, BOTH parties invest their private funds first
  3e. WIDU verifies the private investment has been made
  3f. WIDU grant is released AFTER verification — not before

STEP A-4 — INVESTMENT PLAN REQUIREMENTS (Critical)
  A strong investment plan MUST:
  
  REQUIRED ELEMENTS:
  - Follow chronological, logical business steps
    (example: build chicken coop BEFORE purchasing chicks — not after)
  - Show both donor's and entrepreneur's private investment upfront
  - Quote real, verifiable market prices — over- or under-quoting raises flags
  - Focus on infrastructure and productive assets rather than consumables
  - Demonstrate long-term sustainability and scalability

  COMMON MISTAKES THAT WEAKEN APPLICATIONS:
  - High focus on consumables: stock, lotions, food items, shampoos
    (without investing in the business's underlying infrastructure)
  - Over-budgeting or under-budgeting (credibility issue)
  - Filler budgets: adding unrelated items to reach the minimum threshold
  - Excessive salary or rent expenditure as primary costs
  - Out-of-order steps (e.g., marketing before product is ready)
  - No plan for sustainability 3–6 months after initial investment
  - No scalability vision (should think regionally/nationally, not just locally)

  WHAT WIDU COACHES LOOK FOR:
  - Strategic allocation: Is the money building lasting business capacity?
  - Efficient resource use: Not salary-heavy, not rent-heavy
  - Sustainability: Will this business run without another round of funding?
  - Scalability: Can this grow beyond the local village or county?

STEP A-5 — MONITORING AND ACCOUNTABILITY
  - Transparent record-keeping is mandatory throughout
  - WIDU team conducts verification visits to confirm private investment
  - Grant disbursement is contingent on verified private investment
  - Ongoing reporting required after grant release

STEP A-6 — IMPACT AND SUCCESS FACTORS
  Diaspora-backed WIDU businesses in Kenya have achieved:
  - Scaled-up operations and increased production
  - Creation of meaningful local employment
  - Contribution to sustainable local development

EDGE CASES & EXCEPTIONS
================================================================================
EC-1: WIDU — ENTREPRENEUR IS IN A COUNTRY NOT ON THE ELIGIBLE LIST
  → Current eligible countries: Kenya, Ethiopia, Ghana, Cameroon, Togo, Tunisia
  → If entrepreneur is in Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, etc., WIDU is not currently
    available — check widu.africa for programme expansion updates
  → Suggest alternative: general business mentorship or local angel investors

EC-2: WIDU — DONOR IS IN A NON-ELIGIBLE EUROPEAN COUNTRY
  → Currently eligible: Germany, France, Switzerland, Austria, Netherlands,
    Norway, Belgium, Sweden
  → UK diaspora: Not eligible after Brexit — verify at widu.africa
  → Suggest: Check if a family member/colleague in eligible country can be donor

EC-3: REAL ESTATE — USER WANTS TO BUY IN GERMANY WITHOUT PERMANENT RESIDENCE
  → Possible — there is no citizenship requirement to own real estate in Germany
  → However: getting a German mortgage as a non-resident is very difficult
  → Cash purchase or use of investment fund structures may be alternative
  → Refer to OBN Finance for specific advice

EC-4: WIDU — PRIVATE INVESTMENT ALREADY MADE BUT GRANT NOT RELEASED
  → Most common issue: Investment not properly documented/verified
  → WIDU requires ALL private investment to be made and verified BEFORE grant
  → No partial payments accepted — equipment/assets must be fully paid with
    private investment before WIDU verification begins
  → Advise: Re-check documentation and contact WIDU.africa support

EC-5: SELF-EMPLOYMENT ALONGSIDE EMPLOYMENT (SIDE BUSINESS)
  → Germany allows side businesses (Nebengewerbe or Freiberufliche Nebentätigkeit)
  → Key question: At what income level does it become primary employment?
  → IFB (Institut für Freie Berufe) is the right first contact for this question
  → Tax implications: income from self-employment must be declared regardless

EC-6: AGRI-FOOD PRODUCT — EU IMPORT REGULATIONS
  → Many Kenyan/African agricultural products face EU import restrictions
    (phytosanitary, labelling, certification requirements)
  → Asante Mama case study is a useful reference for what IS possible
  → WTS (global tax advisory, co-presenter at AG-Career Hub event) can advise
    on trade and import regulations

Version: 1.4
Last Updated: 2025-06-01
Department(s): Advisory Services / Community & Events
Related Partners: TH Nürnberg, BVMW, HambraTech, erp4students, Barmer, socialbee,
                  Fintiba, LEMFI, Institut für Freie Berufe

================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================
Guide users on finding, attending, and making the most of AG-Career Hub career
and business networking events, community resources, and mentorship opportunities.
This SOP documents the annual event calendar, event structure, speaker profiles
(by category), and community access channels.

================================================================================
SCOPE
================================================================================
INCLUDED:
- AG-Career Hub annual Career & Business Networking Meet-up (details for 5th edition)
- AG-Career Hub LIVE talks and career success stories
- Community channels: WhatsApp, Telegram
- Mentorship and expert hub
- Career fair events in Kenya (for those planning to come to Germany)

EXCLUDED:
- External German career fairs not organised by AG-Career Hub
- University orientation events (contact respective university directly)
- Events after June 2025 (not yet documented — check ag-careerhub.com)

================================================================================
PREREQUISITES
================================================================================
P-1: For physical attendance: Confirm event registration (tickets on Eventbrite)
P-2: For WhatsApp community: Small subscription fee required
P-3: For LIVE talk access: Free on YouTube/social media (no registration needed)

================================================================================
EDGE CASES & EXCEPTIONS
================================================================================
EC-1: USER OUTSIDE GERMANY WANTS TO ATTEND AN EVENT
  → Annual Career & Business Meet-up is an in-person event in Germany
  → Career Fair in Kenya (2023 edition) shows events are also organised in Kenya
  → LIVE talks on YouTube are accessible globally — recommend these for
    remote participants
  → Annual event location has varied: Kronberg, Frankfurt, Nürnberg — always
    check current year's event page for location

EC-2: USER WANTS TO BE A SPEAKER OR EXHIBITOR
  → Contact AG-Career Hub via the website: info@agcareerhub.com
  → Typically: speakers are professionals from the Kenyan diaspora or German
    organisations with relevant services for the community

EC-3: USER WANTS TO BRING CHILDREN TO THE EVENT
  → Kids corner is provided at the annual event (HambraTech facilitated)
  → Slots for children are LIMITED — must book in advance via the ticket platform
  → Content: STEM activities, art, culture

EC-4: TICKETS ARE SOLD OUT
  → Popular events sell out; monitor Eventbrite page for any returned tickets
  → LIVE talks on YouTube provide free access to similar content
  → WhatsApp community often shares event recaps and resources

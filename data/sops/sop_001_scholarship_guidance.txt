SOP-001: SCHOLARSHIP GUIDANCE & APPLICATIONS
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2025-06-01
Department(s): Advisory Services / FAQ Assistance
Related Programmes: DAAD, KAS, Bundestag IPS, Green Talents, Solitude Fellowship, Helmut-Schmidt

================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================
Guide users from Kenya and the African diaspora through identifying, understanding,
and applying for scholarships to study or conduct research in Germany. This SOP
covers major funded programmes, eligibility criteria, application procedures,
benefit structures, and known limitations in the process.

================================================================================
SCOPE
================================================================================
INCLUDED:
- DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) scholarships for postgraduate study
- Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung (KAS) international scholarships
- German Bundestag International Parliamentary Scholarships (IPS)
- Green Talents Award (BMBF)
- Akademie Schloss Solitude Fellowship
- Helmut-Schmidt-Programme (Public Policy and Good Governance)
- DAAD Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS)

EXCLUDED:
- Undergraduate scholarships (these are rare; direct users to SOP-003)
- Private company sponsorships (refer to SOP-006)
- Sports scholarships (covered in ITK Leipzig content, separate pathway)
- Country-specific scholarships outside the programmes listed above

================================================================================
PREREQUISITES
================================================================================
Before advising on any scholarship application, confirm the user has:
1. A completed first university degree (Bachelor's or equivalent)
2. German language certificate at the relevant level (see SOP-009)
3. A clear study or research objective aligned with the programme
4. Not already started the target degree in Germany (most programmes exclude this)
5. Checked the specific programme's eligible countries list

================================================================================
PROCEDURE
================================================================================

STEP 1 — IDENTIFY THE RIGHT PROGRAMME

Match user profile to programme using this decision matrix:

  PROFILE: STEM graduate, wants Master's in Germany
  → DAAD Study Scholarships for STEM Disciplines
  → Monthly: €992 | Duration: 10–24 months | Start: Oct following year
  → Requirement: Bachelor's by application deadline, B-language skills, from
    developing/emerging country
  → Apply via: https://www2.daad.de (search programme 57742130)

  PROFILE: Music professional, wants postgraduate study at German Hochschule
  → DAAD Study Scholarships – Postgraduate Music
  → Monthly: €992 | Duration: 10–24 months | Includes concert exam option
  → Requirement: First university degree in music OR exhausted training options
    for instrument in home country
  → Selection: DAAD committee of German music professors + sound recording required
  → Note: Separate entrance exam at the Hochschule required; DAAD award does NOT
    guarantee admission

  PROFILE: Graduate with 2+ years professional experience, development focus
  → DAAD Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS)
  → Monthly: €992 (graduates) | €1,300–€1,400 (doctoral candidates, from Feb 2026)
  → Duration: 12–42 months | Travel allowance included
  → Requirement: Bachelor's, 2+ years professional experience, motivation for
    development-related impact
  → Deadline: Varies by programme — check EPOS list at daad.de

  PROFILE: Master's or PhD candidate with political/social engagement
  → Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung (KAS) International Scholarship
  → Target countries (East Africa office): Kenya, Uganda, South Sudan,
    Tanzania, Ethiopia
  → Requirement: B2 German, academic excellence, social/political engagement,
    support for democracy/human rights
  → Benefits: Financial support + international network (15,000+ alumni) +
    seminar programme + personal mentoring
  → Domestic application deadline (in Germany): 15 July, 12:00 noon CET each year
    via KAS Campus Portal (campus.kas.de)
  → Kenya/East Africa direct application deadline: 1 March 2026
    Contact: Ms. Eva-Maria Okot — programm managerin Kenya
  → Official website: https://www.kas.de/en/web/begabtenfoerderung-und-kultur

  PROFILE: Young graduate (<30), politically engaged, B2 German, from East Africa
  → German Bundestag International Parliamentary Scholarships (IPS)
  → Target countries: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda (East Africa); also Ghana, Nigeria,
    Senegal; Botswana, Namibia, South Africa
  → Programme: January programme in Berlin | Core: 1-week internship in Bundestag
    member's office + workshops on democracy, anti-corruption, project management
  → Duration: ~4 weeks (Jan 4 – Feb 2, 2027 for IPS 2027 edition)
  → Stipend: €700/month | Free accommodation | Travel costs covered |
    Health/accident/liability insurance included
  → Requirement: Under 30, completed university studies, B2 German,
    strong political/social commitment
  → Application: Via "IPS digital" online platform (bewerbung.ipsdigital.bundestag.de)
  → Application window for IPS 2027: 1 April – 15 May 2026
  → East Africa (Kenya) past deadline reference: 31 May (check annually)
  → Required documents: Language proof, motivation letter (German, signed, max 2 pages),
    passport copy, degree certificate, recommendation letter
  → Max 24 scholars selected across all participating countries
  → Official page: https://www.bundestag.de/en/europe/international/exchange/ips/africa

  PROFILE: Young researcher in sustainability/environment
  → Green Talents Award (Federal Ministry of Education and Research, BMBF)
  → Benefits: 2-week tour of German sustainability research institutions +
    research stay up to 3 months at German institution of choice +
    rich network of Green Talents alumni + diverse funding for later collaborations
  → More information: https://www.greentalents.de
  → Note: This is a competitive award, not a standard application scholarship

  PROFILE: Artist, scientist, or cultural professional wanting a residential fellowship
  → Akademie Schloss Solitude Fellowship (Stuttgart)
  → Monthly grant: €1,200 | Duration: 6, 9, or 12 months
  → Benefits: Free furnished studio (electricity, water, heating included) +
    travel expenses for arrival/departure
  → Requirement: Not enrolled in undergraduate degree (PhD students may apply) |
    Must reside in Stuttgart minimum 2/3 of fellowship | Register permanent residence
  → Spheres: Visual, Aural/Physical (music, performance), Digital, Spatial
    (architecture, design), Textual (literature), Societal (education, curation),
    Scientific (humanities, social, natural sciences)
  → Selection rounds held every 2 years | Max 65 fellows per round
  → Application: https://www.akademie-solitude.de/en/fellowship
  → Languages: German, English, or French accepted

  PROFILE: Future leader in politics, law, administration from developing country
  → Helmut-Schmidt-Programme (Public Policy and Good Governance / PPGG)
  → Administered by DAAD | Targets developing/emerging countries
  → Funds: Master's degree at German universities in governance-related fields
  → Also covers: Study periods abroad, internships, networking events
  → Programme objective: Train future leaders in democratic, social, and economic
    development for their home countries
  → Track record: 800+ scholars from 100+ countries since 2009 | 95%+ completion rate
  → Official: https://www.daad.de/en/information-services-for-higher-education-institutions/further-information-on-daad-programmes/ppgg/

STEP 2 — CONFIRM LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS
  - All listed scholarships require minimum B2 German
  - DAAD funds language courses for successful scholarship holders before programme start
  - DAAD may fund 2, 4, or 6 months of German language course pre-departure
  - TestDaF and DSH fees can be reimbursed by DAAD during funding period
  - Goethe-Institut certificates are the standard accepted proof
  - Direct user to SOP-009 for detailed language level guidance

STEP 3 — EXPLAIN STANDARD DAAD BENEFIT PACKAGE
  Where applicable, communicate the following standard DAAD benefits:
  - Monthly stipend: €992 (graduates), €1,300–€1,400 (doctoral candidates)
  - Travel allowance (one-time, unless covered by home country)
  - Annual study allowance: €460 (STEM scholarship)
  - Health, accident, and personal liability insurance payments
  - Optional (on request after funding starts): Monthly rent subsidy,
    allowance for accompanying family members
  - Green Mobility Top-Up for climate-friendly travel (some programmes)
  - Note: DAAD does NOT cover tuition fees

STEP 4 — WALK THROUGH APPLICATION PROCESS
  4a. Identify the correct application portal:
      - DAAD scholarships: www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium
      - KAS: campus.kas.de
      - IPS: bewerbung.ipsdigital.bundestag.de
      - Solitude: akademie-solitude.de

  4b. Standard documents required (most programmes):
      - Completed application form
      - Motivation letter (German for most programmes; signed by hand)
      - CV / academic resume
      - University degree certificate (original or certified copy in English/German)
      - Proof of German language proficiency (not older than 2 years for some)
      - Recommendation letters (from lecturer/professor or employer)
      - For music: sound recordings submitted via www.daad.de/extrainfo

  4c. Selection process:
      - Pre-selection from written applications
      - Shortlisted candidates invited to (digital) interview
      - Final decision by independent selection committee
      - Note: For music, a committee of German Hochschule professors decides

STEP 5 — SET TIMELINE EXPECTATIONS
  - Most DAAD scholarships begin 1 October of the following year
  - German language course (if needed) starts earlier
  - KAS domestic deadline: 15 July — applications for following academic year
  - KAS Kenya/East Africa direct: 1 March
  - IPS application window: 1 April – 15 May for following January programme
  - Solitude: Selection rounds every 2 years
  - Green Talents: Annual call, check greentalents.de for current year

================================================================================
EDGE CASES & EXCEPTIONS
================================================================================
EC-1: APPLICANT HAS ALREADY STARTED A MASTER'S IN GERMANY
  → Most DAAD and KAS scholarships cannot be awarded for a degree already started
  → Exception: EPOS may allow continuation in some circumstances — verify with
    DAAD contact
  → Advise user to apply before starting, or look at research fellowships instead

EC-2: APPLICANT HAS A DOCTORAL DEGREE ALREADY
  → DAAD STEM scholarships are for Master's-level only; redirect to postdoctoral
    programmes or Helmut-Schmidt PPGG if policy-focused
  → KAS and Solitude are open to doctoral candidates

EC-3: APPLICANT IS ALREADY IN GERMANY
  → KAS domestic selection: Apply via campus.kas.de by 15 July
  → DAAD: Check whether in-Germany applications are accepted for specific programme
  → IPS: Must apply from home country via embassy in most cases

EC-4: APPLICANT DOES NOT MEET B2 GERMAN REQUIREMENT
  → DAAD funds a language course pre-scholarship, but the scholarship must
    be awarded first
  → Advise user to enrol in a Goethe-Institut course to reach B2 before applying
  → Estimated timeline to B2 from zero: 12–18 months of study
  → See SOP-009 for language pathways

EC-5: MUSIC APPLICANT FAILS HOCHSCHULE ENTRANCE EXAM
  → DAAD award does not guarantee admission to any specific Hochschule
  → Applicant should research entrance exam requirements and application
    deadlines BEFORE DAAD award (these may be several months before DAAD deadline)
  → If rejected from all chosen Hochschulen, DAAD scholarship cannot be used
  → Advise: apply to multiple Hochschulen simultaneously

EC-6: DUAL CITIZENSHIP — KAS AND SOLITUDE
  → Solitude: Dual citizenship possible, but check entry/residence regulations
    for partners and families — not always possible to accommodate families abroad
  → KAS: Dual citizenship acceptable if one nationality is from eligible country
  → IPS: Dual citizenship explicitly allowed

EC-7: APPLICANT FROM NON-LISTED COUNTRY
  → Check each programme's eligible country list carefully
  → EPOS has a specific eligible countries list on DAAD website
  → IPS is limited to 9 sub-Saharan African countries only
  → If country not eligible: suggest Erasmus+, bilateral agreements, or
    university-specific scholarships

EC-8: PHD STUDENT APPLYING FOR SOLITUDE
  → PhD students may apply; enrolled undergraduate students may not
  → Fellowship requires physical residence in Stuttgart — not compatible with
    a German university PhD programme unless very flexible arrangement

================================================================================
SYSTEM LIMITATIONS (DATA & PROCESS CONSTRAINTS)
================================================================================
SL-1: DEADLINE CURRENCY
  → Scholarship deadlines change annually; all dates in this SOP reflect 2025/2026
    cycle. Always verify at official programme websites before advising.
  → IPS past patterns: East Africa (Kenya) deadline historically 31 May;
    2026 deadline confirmed as 15 May for IPS 2027

SL-2: EPOS PROGRAMME LIST
  → The EPOS Development-Related Postgraduate Courses list changes each cycle
  → Always direct users to the official DAAD EPOS list PDF for current
    programmes with application deadlines

SL-3: STIPEND AMOUNTS
  → DAAD stipend amounts are subject to periodic adjustment
  → €1,400/month for doctoral candidates effective February 2026 (was €1,300)
  → Verify current amounts at daad.de before quoting

SL-4: KAS KENYA OFFICE CONTACT
  → Contact for Kenya office is Ms. Eva-Maria Okot
  → Email protected on website; direct users to the official KAS Kenya page
    for current contact details

SL-5: SOLITUDE SELECTION CYCLE
  → Fellowship rounds are every 2 years; if a round just closed, the next
    opportunity may be 2 years away
  → Website does not always display the next open round prominently

================================================================================
ESCALATION CRITERIA
================================================================================
ESCALATE to human advisory or refer to AG-Career Hub consultation service when:
- User has specific combination of factors not covered by this SOP
  (e.g., dual citizenship, mid-programme transfer, disability accommodation)
- User has been shortlisted or awarded and needs detailed next-step guidance
- Scholarship application was rejected and user wants appeal/reapplication advice
- User has country-specific questions about embassy procedures for IPS
- Questions about the KAAD (Catholic Academic Exchange Service) PhD scholarship
  — limited public information, direct to https://www.kaad.de
